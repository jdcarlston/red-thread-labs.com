concepts:
  - term: The curtain
    def: The performance that hides an ordinary truth.
  - term: Unconditioned seeing
    def: Noticing what’s there without a stake in the story.
  - term: Small, not powerful
    def: Revelation often comes from the least impressive place.
  - term: Free contact
    def: Meeting what’s real, directly.
context: "Toto stands for a move the labs practice: stop performing, notice what’s actually here, and let the curtain fall. It’s JD’s mythic reading, a teaching image rather than research."
exercise:
  title: Behind the curtain
  time: About 15 minutes
  steps:
    - "Think of something you’re intimidated by: a person, an institution, a voice in your head."
    - "Write the “great and terrible” version: how it seems."
    - Now look plainly, like a small dog sniffing around. What is actually there? Who is pulling the levers?
    - Write what you see.
    - Notice how your body feels after the curtain drops.
  why: Toto’s moment is about seeing past an imposing performance to the ordinary reality. Describing both versions side by side often shrinks the intimidation to its real size.
