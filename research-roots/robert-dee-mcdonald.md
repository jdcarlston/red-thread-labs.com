exercise:
  title: Helping and being helped
  time: About 15 minutes
  steps:
    - List the people you help regularly, and how.
    - List the people who help you, and how.
    - Notice whether one list is much longer, and how you feel about that.
    - Pick one person you could let help you a little more this week, and one small thing to ask for.
    - Afterward, note what it was like to receive.
  why: Research on relationships and well-being consistently finds that support given and received both matter, and that people who only give can wear themselves out. Making both lists shows where the balance has tipped.
