concepts:
  - term: Start with heart
    def: Clarify what you really want before you speak.
  - term: Silence or violence
    def: The two ways people leave honest dialogue under stress.
  - term: Make it safe
    def: Restore shared purpose and mutual respect.
  - term: Contrasting
    def: “I don’t mean… I do mean…” to repair a misunderstanding.
  - term: Master your stories
    def: What you saw, the story you told about it, how you felt, what you did.
context: "These tools translate well from the boardroom to the dinner table. In the labs we use the silence-or-violence check alongside Gottman’s Four Horsemen, and contrasting as a way out of defensiveness. Mastering your stories is the same lever we return to often: the story you tell yourself is where change can start."
exercise:
  title: Start with heart
  time: About 10 minutes, before a hard conversation
  steps:
    - Write down the conversation you need to have.
    - "Answer three questions: What do I really want for me? For them? For us?"
    - "Ask: How would I behave if I really wanted those things?"
    - "Write one contrasting sentence: “I don’t want you to think… What I do want is…”"
    - Go into the conversation with these written down, and check yourself for silence or aggression as you go.
  why: The authors found that skilled communicators clarify their real goals before high-stakes talks and protect the other person’s sense of safety. Writing your goals down makes it less likely you’ll drift into winning or withdrawing.
