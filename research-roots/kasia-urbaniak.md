concepts:
  - term: Attention as power
    def: Where you put your attention, outward or inward, shapes the power in a room.
  - term: Good-girl conditioning
    def: Being taught to shrink, avoid conflict and hide desire, anger and fear.
  - term: Leveling
    def: Apologizing, hedging and over-explaining that quietly give power away.
  - term: The direct ask
    def: Saying what you want plainly instead of hinting or complaining.
context: "Urbaniak gives practical tools for something the labs care about: asking for what you want and handling the answer. Her teaching is aimed at women, but the skills apply to anyone taught that wanting is dangerous."
exercise:
  title: One clean ask
  time: A few minutes, once a day for a week
  steps:
    - "Each day, make one small, direct request: “Will you pass the salt?” “Can you call me tonight?” “I’d like the window seat.”"
    - Say it without apologizing, hedging or explaining why you deserve it.
    - Notice the urge to add “sorry” or “if it’s okay,” and let it pass.
    - Whatever the answer, say “Thank you” and let it be.
    - At the end of the week, write down what got easier.
  why: Urbaniak teaches that plain requests are a skill built through practice, and that the habit of softening every ask quietly undermines it. Starting with small, low-stakes asks builds the muscle for bigger ones.
